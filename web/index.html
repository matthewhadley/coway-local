Connect your fan

Open a setup link from Home Assistant to begin.

Put the fan in Wi-Fi setup mode and close the Coway app. Then choose it in Chrome’s device picker. Devices are not filtered by name.

Tested with AP-1022K. Other models are experimental and must use the same setup protocol.
